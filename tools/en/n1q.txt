 一、文法測驗
 1. （ ）I was playing computer games, and my sisters ＿＿＿, too.
       (A) are (B) was (C) were (D) do
 2. （ ）Jack was writing an e-mail to his teacher ＿＿＿.
       (A) every morning (B) at ten last night (C) now (D) on weekends
 3. （ ）Lisa was playing the guitar ＿＿＿ her friend called this morning.
       (A) when (B) after (C) because (D) and
 4. （ ）A：What ______ you doing when Mom called you?
       B：I ______ playing chess with Grandfather.
       (A) are; am (B) were; were (C) are; was (D) were; was
 5. （ ）A：What was John ＿＿＿ then?
       B：He ＿＿＿ washing his car.
       (A) do; be (B) did; were (C) does; is (D) doing; was
 6. （ ）A：What ＿＿＿ Nancy doing when you entered?
       B：She ＿＿＿ cleaning the table when I entered.
       (A) was; was (B) is; is (C) were; were (D) does; did
 7. （ ）A：What were these students ＿＿＿ at six yesterday evening?
       B：They ＿＿＿ basketball.
       (A) do; practicing      (B) doing; were practicing
       (C) does; practiced     (D) doing; are practicing
 8. （ ）A：Did you see Eric at nine this morning?
       B：Yes, he ＿＿＿ in his bedroom then.
       (A) is sleeping (B) sleeps (C) was sleeping (D) slept
 9. （ ）Sorry, I didn’t answer the phone one hour ago. When you ______, I ______ a bath
       （泡澡）.
       (A) were calling; took (B) call; was taking
       (C) called; was taking (D) called; am taking
10. （ ）A：What were you ______ when I ______ the house? It smelled（聞起來）delicious.
       B：It was chicken curry（咖哩）. Mom made this, not me.
       (A) cooking; entering (B) eating; entered
       (C) cooking; enter      (D) eating; enter
11. （ ）A：______ was my dog doing when you saw it?
       B：It ______ after a cat at the playground.
       (A) Where; ran (B) Where; was running
       (C) What; ran (D) What; was running
12. （ ）A：What ＿＿＿ you doing when your mom called?
       B：I ＿＿＿ flowers when she called.
       (A) did; water (B) was; watering
       (C) are; watering (D) were; was watering
13. （ ）When I ______ the strange noise, my dog was ______ out of the window. However,
       there was nothing outside（外面）.
       (A) heard; looking (B) heard; looked (C) hear; looking (D) hear; looked
14. （ ）A：Why ＿＿＿ you ＿＿＿ at Elvis last night?
       B：Because he sat on my glasses and broke（打破）them.
       (A) did; shout (B) was; shouting (C) do; shout (D) are; shouting
