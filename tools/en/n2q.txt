 一、文法測驗
 1. （ ）Carol ＿＿＿ a lot of water after she exercises every day.
             (A) drink (B) is drinking (C) drinks (D) drank
 2. （ ）When Mr. Li shouted, the baby（嬰兒）＿＿＿.
             (A) cry (B) cried (C) cries (D) is crying
 3. （ ）＿＿＿ Mr. and Mrs. Wang lost their daughter, they were sad.
             (A) Or (B) However (C) After (D) So
 4. （ ）Sandy and I were classmates when ______ in junior high school. We ______ next to each
             other（彼此）for three years.
             (A) we studied; sat (B) studied; sit (C) we study; sat (D) study; sit
 5. （ ）My mom always prepares breakfast for me before I ＿＿＿ for school. She is really nice to
             me.
             (A) left (B) leave (C) leaving (D) am leaving
 6. （ ）Tom, put down the chopsticks（筷子）. ______ you eat dinner, you should ______ your hands.
             (A) After; washed (B) Before; wash (C) When; washed (D) Before; washing
 7. （ ）When my parents ＿＿＿ young, they ＿＿＿ work and study at the same time. That was
             really hard.
             (A) are; have to (B) was; must (C) were; had to (D) is; has to
 8. （ ）We practiced basketball for one hour ______ then talked for another（另一個的）hour ______
             Jay arrived（到達）. That is, he was two hours late.
             (A) and; after (B) ╳; after (C) and; before (D) so; before
 9. （ ）A：Ivy ______ to our house ______ you went to bed last night.
             B：Really? Why didn’t you wake me up then?
             (A) comes; when (B) came; before (C) comes; because (D) came; after
10. （ ）Before Timothy went to the movies, he ＿＿＿ the computer for me. I really thanked him.
             (A) fix (B) fixed (C) fixes (D) is fixing
11. （ ）Carl ＿＿＿ the flowers after he cleaned the classroom this afternoon. He did a lot for his
             class.
             (A) waters (B) watered (C) water (D) is watering
12. （ ）A：Wow! James is a famous actor now.
             B：Yes, he is. However, he failed many times ＿＿＿ he succeeded（成功）.
             (A) and (B) but (C) before (D) after
