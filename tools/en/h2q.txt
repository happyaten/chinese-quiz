 一、文法測驗
 1. （ ）When I got home, my mom ＿＿＿ in her room.
       (A) is sleeping (B) sleeps (C) sleep (D) was sleeping
 2. （ ）Betty           fake watches when the police came.【90 基測】
       (A) sells (B) is selling (C) was selling (D) has sold
 3. （ ）A：Why ＿＿＿ Eddy ＿＿＿ then?
       B：His wife left him, so he was very sad.
       (A) was; crying (B) is; crying       (C) do; cry (D) does; cry
 4. （ ）A：Why ＿＿＿ you shouting then?
       B：I ＿＿＿ a terrible dream.
       (A) are; have (B) were; had        (C) did; was having (D) was; has
 5. （ ）A：What ＿＿＿ Allen ＿＿＿ at seven yesterday evening?
       B：He was studying in the library.
       (A) was; doing (B) is; doing (C) did; do (D) does; do
 6. （ ）Betty           TV when her little brother fell off the chair.【97 基測】
       (A) watched (B) was watching (C) has watched (D) is going to watch
 7. （ ）A：______ the cute rabbit ______ around in the park when you were there?
       B：No, it wasn’t. In fact, I didn’t see it today.
       (A) Did; jump (B) Is; jumping         (C) Was; jumping (D) Does; jump
 8. （ ）A：Guess what! I met your favorite boy Larry at the gym.
       B：Really? What ______ he ______ there? Practicing basketball?
       (A) was; doing (B) did; do (C) is; doing (D) does; do
 9. （ ）I          a bath when someone turned off the light. I was scared and shouted for help.
       (A) was taking (B) took (C) am taking (D) had taken
10. （ ）A：You didn’t answer the phone this morning.
       B：Sorry. I ＿＿＿ when you called.
       (A) am swimming (B) swim (C) was swimming (D) swimming
11. （ ）A：What ______ you ______ at 10 a.m. yesterday?
       B：I was busy with work. I didn’t even（甚至）have time for lunch.
       (A) were; doing (B) were; do         (C) are; doing (D) do; do
12. （ ）A：What ______ your sister ______ at 2 p.m. yesterday?
       B：I don’t know. I didn’t see her until（直到）4 p.m.
       (A) did; do (B) is; doing       (C) was; doing (D) were; doing
13. （ ）A：I went to Joe’s place two hours ago, but when I saw him, he seemed（似乎）very tired.
       B：Really? ______ was Joe doing ______?
       A：His science report（報告）.
       (A) What; then (B) What; last night         (C) How; then (D) How; yesterday
14. （ ）I couldn’t do my homework at that time because Jessie ＿＿＿ much noise. She was
       mopping the floor then.
       (A) were making (B) was making           (C) is making (D) makes
15. （ ）A：Is it three thirty now?
       B：Yes, it’s ______ three. (A) half to (B) half past (C) a half after (D) past a half
16. （ ）A：I usually get up at eight fifteen from Monday to Friday.
       B：Do you get up at ______ eight on weekends, too?
       A：No, I usually get up at ______ on weekends.
       (A) a quarter past; eight forty-five           (B) a quarter to; a quarter to nine
       (C) a quarter after; eight and a quarter       (D) a quarter before; eight forty-five
