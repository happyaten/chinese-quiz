 一、文法測驗
 1. （ ）My brother and his friends like ＿＿＿ the guitar.
        (A) play (B) plays (C) played (D) to play
 2. （ ）Jerry wants ＿＿＿ money for a new computer.
        (A) save (B) saving (C) to save (D) to saving
 3. （ ）A：It’s nice of you to help me ＿＿＿ my math homework.
        B：No problem.
        (A) to (B) in (C) of (D) with
 4. （ ）I want to ＿＿＿ Shirley. She is beautiful and smart.
        (A) be to like (B) be like (C) being like (D) likes
 5. （ ）A：Was your dog swimming when I saw you at the beach?
        B：Yes, it ______. It loves ______ and play frisbee a lot.
        (A) was; to swim (B) was; swim (C) is; to swim (D) did; swim
 6. （ ）Sorry, I ______ go home now because it’s 11 p.m. ______ dangerous（危險的）to stay out late
        at night.
        (A) need; It’s (B) have to; Its (C) should; It’s (D) want to; Its
 7. （ ）My sister wants ＿＿＿ a school teacher. She prepares for the test almost（幾乎）every day.
        (A) be (B) to be (C) being (D) to being
 8. （ ）My cousin loves ＿＿＿. She is especially（尤其）good at cooking beef noodles.
        (A) cook (B) cooks (C) cooked (D) to cook
 9. （ ）A：Why ______ you go to the party last night? Don’t you like parties?
        B：Yes, I do. But I needed ______ care of my brother because my parents weren’t home then.
        (A) did; to take (B) don’t; to take (C) didn’t; took (D) didn’t; to take
10. （ ）Mom：Judy, we need ＿＿＿ some water. A typhoon（颱風）is coming.
        Judy：I see, Mom.
        (A) save (B) saving (C) to save (D) saved
11. （ ）Dad always tells me not           only for tests. If that’s all I’m doing, he says, I will soon lose
        interest in learning.
        (A) study (B) studying (C) studied (D) to study
12. （ ）A：Thanks ＿＿＿ your help. Thanks ＿＿＿ you, I finished my work on time.
        B：Don’t mention it（別客氣）.
        (A) for; for (B) for; to (C) to; to (D) to; for
13. （ ）Before Joe ______ abroad（國外）to work, he planned ______ English well. Now it’s easy for
        him to talk to foreigners（外國人）in English.
        (A) goes; to learn (B) went; to learn (C) goes; learning (D) went; learned
14. （ ）______ your hands before meals. It’s not good ______ with your dirty hands.
        (A) To wash ; to eat (B) Wash; to eat (C) Washing; eating (D) Wash; eat
